# pookdevs: Sweetopia

A Roblox bakery tycoon set around a town square. Players bake treats from ingredients, stock display cases, sell to customers who shop on their own, and pack deliveries for coins and Sugar Stars. The plaza is a spring park, with a three-tier marble fountain whose jets dance in patterns over the sound of running water, cherry trees dropping petals, green trees, planters and flower beds on a grass lawn. Townsfolk are Roblox avatars in Roblox's free hair and clothes, who walk in from the edges of town with Roblox's own walk and stroll around the plaza. Customers are townsfolk who head in from the plaza at random moments, with a thought bubble showing the unlocked treat they want. They tip when they get it and settle for another treat when it's missing, then go back to the plaza. Normally a customer comes every 12–18 seconds, depending on Charm, with the odd quick follow-up or quiet spell, and up to 3 at a time. Boosts bring more, from the Boosts menu: a free Bake Sale once an hour (25% off and 50% more customers for 2 minutes, with a banner over the door), flyers from a stand outside the door, carried as a stack in your hand, that send townsfolk you walk past to your bakery, Bakery Buzz for 3 Sugar Stars, and the 2x Boost, which doubles customers as well as coins. A hired Baker bakes a random treat you have ingredients for. The checkout counter is the size of the other counters, with a retro cash register. Ingredients come from a free delivery crate and the grocery store on the square. A Walk/Run switch beside the hotbar lets anyone run, at speed 28 instead of 16. Coins and Stars pay for upgrades, recipes and decorations. Owners of a pack can buy more of its decorations with coins, but only the first of each adds Charm. Everything in a bakery, including the oven and counters, can be moved and turned on a grid of 1-stud squares. Wall decorations, such as sconces, plants and paintings, hang on the back and side walls and have their own Wall tab in the Decorate menu. The Decorate menu's Paint tab recolours each wall, the oven and each display case. Its Expansion tab makes the bakery bigger in three steps, and sells the gold Luxury Oven, a second oven for 25,000 coins that bakes 15% faster and can't be repainted. Players can bake together: anyone can ask at another bakery's door, and once its owner says yes, they bake with the owner's ingredients, fill the owner's cases and pack orders, with a 👫 Friends menu to stop, remove co-bakers or invite friends. Each sale pays the owner 10% more for each active co-baker and gives each of them 25% of the price. Roblox friends in the same town get a Friend Boost of +10% coins each, up to +30%, and a friend's first visit through an invite gives both players 10 Sugar Stars. Every game pass and Robux product has its own frosted badge icon, shown in Roblox's purchase window and beside each row of the in-game Get More menu.

The `.server.luau` and `.client.luau` names follow Rojo's convention for a Script and a LocalScript. Files ending in plain `.luau` are ModuleScripts.

## Restoring from this backup
1. Open the place file from `backups/` in Studio with **File → Open from File**.
2. For each file in `src/`, open the matching script from the table above in Studio. If it's missing, add it: a LocalScript for `.client.luau`, a ModuleScript for plain `.luau`.
3. Replace that script's text with the file's contents.
4. Run each builder in `tools/` from the Studio command bar, in this order: oven, cookbook, pantry, town, fountain, greenery, townsfolk, layout, checkout, luxury oven.
5. Publish with **File → Save to Roblox**, or **File → Publish to Roblox** if the place is new.
