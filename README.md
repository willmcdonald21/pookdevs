# pookdevs: Sweetopia

A Roblox bakery tycoon set around a town square. Players bake treats from ingredients, stock display cases, sell to customers who shop on their own and line up at the register to pay, and pack deliveries for coins and Sugar Stars. The plaza is a spring park, with a three-tier marble fountain whose jets dance in patterns over the sound of running water, cherry trees dropping petals, green trees, planters and flower beds on a grass lawn. Townsfolk are Roblox avatars in Roblox's free hair and clothes, who walk in from the edges of town with Roblox's own walk and stroll around the plaza. Customers are townsfolk who head in from the plaza at random moments, with a thought bubble showing the unlocked treat they want. They tip when they get it and settle for another treat when it's missing, then go back to the plaza. Normally a customer comes every 12–18 seconds, depending on Charm, with the odd quick follow-up or quiet spell, and no limit on how many are inside at once. After paying, customers sit for 30 to 60 seconds at a random free seat, on a Cafe Table's stools or an Armchair, before leaving. Boosts bring more, from the Boosts menu: a free Bake Sale once an hour (25% off and 50% more customers for 2 minutes, with a banner over the door), flyers from a stand outside the door, carried as a stack in your hand, that send townsfolk you walk past to your bakery, Bakery Buzz for 3 Sugar Stars, and the 2x Boost, which doubles customers as well as coins. The checkout counter is the size of the other counters, with a retro cash register. Customers wait in a single-file line in front of it and pay when the player taps E there, or when a Cashier checks them out. Up to two employees, hired in the Employees menu for 200 and then 600 coins, each do a different job: Cashier, Baker (bakes a random treat you have ingredients for) or Stocker (carries batches to the cases). Training one for 1,000 makes them faster. Each wears the bakery apron and a name tag, and is dressed and named in a dressing room laid out like the Roblox avatar editor, from Roblox's free skin tones, hair, shirts, pants and hats. The Delivery and Cookbook counters open their menus, with a bouncing ! when something there is ready. Ingredients come from a free delivery crate and the grocery store on the square, which sells bundles of 5, a fill-up button for each ingredient, and Buy all, which fills the whole pantry after a box shows the total coins. A Walk/Run switch beside the hotbar lets anyone run, at speed 28 instead of 16. Coins and Stars pay for upgrades, recipes and decorations. Owners of a pack can buy more of its decorations with coins, but only the first of each adds Charm. Everything in a bakery, including the oven and counters, can be moved and turned on a grid of 1-stud squares. Picking up a decoration or display case shows Sell, which pays back half its price in the coins or Stars it cost after a second "Sure?" tap, and Inventory, which puts it away. Put-away pieces are listed in the Decorate menu's Inventory tab, to place or sell. VIP pieces, the first copy of a pack piece and the last display case can't be sold. Wall decorations, such as sconces, plants and paintings, hang on the back and side walls and have their own Wall tab in the Decorate menu. The Decorate menu's Paint tab recolours each wall, the oven and each display case. Buying a display case asks for its colour first, free with the case, and a case put away keeps its colour. The Paint tab also tiles the floor in 4-stud squares, one tile at a time or the whole floor at once, for 1 or 2 coins a tile: oak, walnut or cherry planks, white or pink marble, grey slate, five pastel ceramic tiles, or a black-and-white or pink-and-white checkerboard. Laying the original cream planks takes tiles up for free. Its Expansion tab makes the bakery bigger in three steps, and sells the gold Luxury Oven, a second oven for 5,000 coins that bakes 15% faster and can't be repainted. The Sweet Stash, a one-time 1,000-coin Shop upgrade, opens a storage room through a door in the back wall. Its whiteboard sells Stash Shelves, open wooden racks for 60 coins that hold 12 treats and only go in the stash, and has Customize store shelves, where tapping a display case chooses the one treat the Stocker puts in it. The Stocker puts treats with no case to go to on the shelves and brings them out when a case has room. Customers never shop from the shelves, and a shelf's Take Treats prompt loads them onto your tray. Tapping your own bakery's sign in the plaza opens its outside menu. Its title is the bakery's name, which you tap to rename, up to 24 characters, filtered by Roblox before it shows on the sign. Its Colours tab paints the outside of the walls (20 coins) and the trim, sign and posts (10 coins) in any paint colour, while the inside paint stays inside, and going back to the original colours is free. Its Garden tab sells a 1,600-coin garden on the lawn around the bakery, and then luxury plants to place in it like decorations: a Flower Patch, Tulip Row, Round Bush, Flowering Bush, Potted Topiary, Box Hedge, Little Green Tree and Rose Arch for 80 to 500 coins and 2 to 4 Charm, and the deluxe Heart Topiary, Cherry Blossom Tree (with falling petals) and Golden Flower Bed for 20, 30 and 40 Sugar Stars and 10, 12 and 15 Charm. Plants keep clear of the path, the crate and flyer stand, the plaza, the townsfolk's ways in and out of town and the neighbours' gardens, and any in the way when the bakery grows or the stash is bought go to the Inventory. Players can bake together: anyone can ask at another bakery's door, and once its owner says yes, they bake with the owner's ingredients, fill the owner's cases and pack orders, with a 👫 Friends menu to stop, remove co-bakers or invite friends. Each sale pays the owner 10% more for each active co-baker and gives each of them 25% of the price. Roblox friends in the same town get a Friend Boost of +10% coins each, up to +30%, and a friend's first visit through an invite gives both players 10 Sugar Stars. Every sale raises the bakery's Bakery Level, shown as a badge with an XP bar under Boosts. XP is each treat's base price, so boosts and 2x Coins don't speed it up. Cookie is free and Cupcake, Donut, Pie and Birthday Cake are bought in the Cookbook, while seven more treats are learned by levelling up, with a Level up! popup: Croissant (level 6), Cinnamon Roll (7), Macaron (8), Éclair (10), Cheesecake (12), Strawberry Shortcake (13) and Wedding Cake (15, the top level). Each Shop upgrade has four more levels past the old maximum, costing 1,200 to 48,000 coins and needing Bakery Level 6, 9, 12 and 14. Treats sell for 1 coin (Cookie) up to 100 (Wedding Cake), and each tier up bakes longer, from 5 seconds for a batch of Cookies to 50 for Wedding Cakes at the starting oven. Prices round to the nearest coin. At the top level, with 2 employees hired, the Cookbook's Open a new restaurant starts a new one from scratch, with its own coins, level, recipes, upgrades, decor and employees, while Sugar Stars and passes stay with the player. Every restaurant after the first gets 20% more customers. The restaurants you aren't managing run by themselves on free plots, just for show, with three workers and nothing saved. The newest go on show first, and a player joining always gets a plot ahead of them. Walking up to one's door shows Manage this bakery, only to its owner, which swaps it in after a Yes. Every game pass and Robux product has its own frosted badge icon, shown in Roblox's purchase window and beside each row of the in-game Get More menu.

## Restoring from this backup
1. Open the place file from `backups/` in Studio with **File → Open from File**.
2. For each file in `src/`, open the matching script from the table above in Studio. If it's missing, add it: a LocalScript for `.client.luau`, a ModuleScript for plain `.luau`.
3. Replace that script's text with the file's contents.
4. Run each builder in `tools/` from the Studio command bar, in this order: oven, cookbook, pantry, town, fountain, greenery, townsfolk, layout, checkout, luxury oven.
5. Publish with **File → Save to Roblox**, or **File → Publish to Roblox** if the place is new.
