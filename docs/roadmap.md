## Not yet verified
- **Purchase window:** all passes and products exist and are connected, and a simulated receipt paid out and saved correctly. Studio wouldn't show Roblox's purchase window, so the first full purchase will be in the published game.
- **Prompts:** during the 2026-11-05 tests, Studio wouldn't show any proximity prompt, even a new test prompt. The oven, cases, cookbook counter, pantry shelf, crate and grocery counter were tested by calling the same server code directly, not by pressing E.
- **Decorating on a phone:** Studio's simulator doesn't turn simulated clicks into taps, so tapping the floor to move the preview, tapping a wall to move a wall piece, and tapping a piece to pick it up are untested. The mouse controls were tested. The four Decorate tabs shrink to share the bar on narrow screens, but haven't been seen on a phone.
- **A bakery going back to its default layout** when its owner leaves while another player is in the server.
- **Live play:** two players at once, a real phone, and a full playthrough at normal prices.
- **Living plaza visuals:** on 2026-11-07, Play screenshots showed the strolling crowd as Roblox avatars, a thought bubble, a golden customer's crown, and customers shopping and tipping. The new cash register still hasn't been looked at.
- **The plaza on a phone:** up to about 20 avatars (a few with 3D clothes), the fountain's particles and beams, and about 1,300 park parts haven't been tried on a real phone. If it's slow, the first things to cut are the layered clothes in `build_townsfolk`, the lawn's tree count in `build_greenery`, and the fountain's splash rates.
- **A busy plaza:** the crowd with several bakeries open at once. Customers walk from the plaza, so each visit takes a few seconds longer. There's no limit on customers in a bakery any more, so with boosts stacked a bakery can fill up; the line at the register is only as long as the stock in the cases.
- **Boosts:** tested in Studio on 2026-11-07 with arrival and coin logs and screenshots. Normal gaps ran 12–23 seconds, sale prices and the banner were right, a second sale within the hour was refused, Buzz took 3 Stars, and boosts stacked to 3x. Flyer hand-outs sent people to the bakery. Not covered: taking flyers was tested by firing the same remote the prompt fires, not by pressing E, and the 2x Boost by setting its end time, not by buying it. On a small phone, the Boosts button (now the fourth in the left column) may sit close to the thumbstick.

- **Bake Together:**
  - **Tested solo in Studio on 2026-11-07:** a single player still unpacks crates, the helpers bake and stock, customers pay whole coins, and the save gets the new invite fields. The Friends menu and the request card were checked in screenshots.
  - **Not yet tested:** two players baking together, the door prompt on another player's bakery, the Friend Boost between real friends, and invite rewards, which need the published game and Creator Hub's Friend Referral set up.
- **Employees:**
  - **Tested on 2026-11-07** in Studio on a throwaway save: the line at the register, checking out with E, hiring, training, swapping jobs, the Cashier's timing, dressing and renaming, and moving the counter with a line.
  - **Not yet tested:** a co-baker at the register and at a friend's cookbook counter, an old save loading into employees in the published game, employees surviving a rejoin, and the dressing room on a real phone.
  - **The ! over the counters** doesn't draw on top of walls, because Studio screenshots leave such markers out. It may be hidden behind a wall when you're outside the bakery.
- **Script size:** `BakerySystem` and `BakeryClient` are close to Luau's limit of 200 local variables at the top of a script. After the employees change on 2026-11-07, BakerySystem had room for 15 more and BakeryClient for 5. Put new sections in functions, so their locals don't count. A `do ... end` block helps less: its locals and loop variables still count while it runs, and in BakeryClient a block with seven named constants and two loops ran out of room.

## Before launch
- Set the server size to 6 players, since there are six bakeries, and keep 2 slots for friends (Server Fill) so friends can join to bake together.
- Have 2–3 people who haven't seen the game play it without help.

## Money notes
- **Creator share:** creators receive 70% of Robux spent.
- **Cashing out:** DevEx needs at least 30,000 Earned Robux, which is about $114. That requires a verified email, a DevEx account, and a tax form.
- **Audience:** publishing to the 16+ audience is free. Kids and Select need a fee or a subscription, plus an evaluation.
